## Final prompt

Create a single photorealistic-natural concept image for an internal UNDRR
design-system communications lookbook. Landscape 3:2 editorial documentary
photograph, realistic natural daytime light, restrained colours, no stylised
filters. Subject: a small diverse community preparedness team actively mapping
local flood risk outdoors beside a modest neighbourhood lane with greenery and
simple homes; two women and one man of varied ages discussing a large paper map
on a wooden table, an older woman participates as an expert; one participant
uses a manual wheelchair naturally and actively at the table, without being
singled out. Show attentive collaboration and practical action, dignity and
agency, everyday attire. Mid-distance eye-level candid composition; people and
map occupy central third so the image survives both tall and wide crops, enough
surrounding neighbourhood context. Fictional non-identifiable people, faces in
three-quarter view or profile, no resemblance to known individuals. The map is
a believable simple unlabelled plan, no words or numbers. NO text, logos,
emblems, flags, office lanyards, conference scenes, disaster destruction,
emergency drama, stock-photo handshakes or heroic posing. This is a fictional
illustrative scene, not evidence of a real event. Generate only the photograph,
no layout, no border, no watermark.
