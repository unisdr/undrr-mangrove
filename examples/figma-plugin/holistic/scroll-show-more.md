## Required subtree mask capability

The source mask is `linear-gradient(to bottom, black 120px, transparent 200px)`, with a 200px maximum target height. Some actual React targets are only 168px tall. Their fade still ends at 200px; normalizing the gradient to the shorter target would visibly change the source. A source-equivalent mask must therefore keep its 200px gradient extent while clipping to the actual target height.

The mask applies to target background and text together. The Default story has a transparent target over an unmasked tan caller. Detached/two-block vanilla targets have their own tan background, which fades with their text. The toggle is outside the mask owner and must remain opaque.

The reviewed source-candidate ALPHA mask uses an isolated clipping owner, first fixed 200px absolute mask FRAME and subsequent genuine editable content subtree. FRAME, RECTANGLE and documented GROUP cloud prototypes did not fade rendered pixels despite retaining mask flags and gradient properties. Native rendered semantics remain failed or unproved. The guarded source candidate and its actual-builder ownership tests are documented in [ALPHA-MASK-CAPABILITY.md](ALPHA-MASK-CAPABILITY.md); its connector exceeds the unchanged transport ceiling. Expanded appearances declare a transparent NONE reset with the same anatomy identities. No background wash or screenshot is being substituted for reusable masked content.

Official references: [Shared node properties](https://developers.figma.com/docs/plugins/api/node-properties/) and [MaskType](https://developers.figma.com/docs/plugins/api/MaskType/).
