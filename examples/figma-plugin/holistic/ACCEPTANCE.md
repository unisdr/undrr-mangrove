3. Stable identities, two zero-create mock repeats and supported edited content.
4. Rendered source comparison at declared args, dimensions, brand and script.
5. Isolated native construction and structural/visual parity at that same scope.
6. Two ordinary native zero-create repeats with exact recorded geometry and
   reusable-instance edit retention.

32-family catalogue remain bounded contracts. The recorded 25-family published
checkpoint is historical evidence, not a fresh holistic validation.
