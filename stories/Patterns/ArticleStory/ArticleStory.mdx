# Article story

> **Note:** Reference design for [undrr/web-backlog#3060](https://gitlab.com/undrr/web-backlog/-/work_items/3060). Review against the [component checklist](https://github.com/unisdr/undrr-mangrove/blob/main/docs/REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md).

A full article-page pattern (not just a header): headline, hero image, share,
body, related content, recommendations, taxonomy tags and feedback. It is a
reference implementation for comparing hero treatments in
[undrr/web-backlog#3060](https://gitlab.com/undrr/web-backlog/-/work_items/3060).

- **Header prominence** (`imageProminence`): `large` (full bleed), `compact`
  (reading-column width), `split` (text and image side-by-side).
- **Header image source** (`heroImage`): `main`, `alternate`, or `none`,
  independent of teaser/social image choices.

Fixture content is based on a real undrr.org story:
[Towards knowledge, action and resolve to address non-economic losses in the Pacific Indigenous Communities Nadi, Fiji](https://www.undrr.org/news/towards-knowledge-action-and-resolve-address-non-economic-losses-pacific-indigenous).
Metadata and media are reproduced from a saved copy; body text is placeholder
content sized for realistic reading-column evaluation.

## Building one

Composes existing pieces (`Hero`, `TableOfContents`, `ShareButtons`,
`VerticalCard`, `Imagecaption`, `UserFeedback`, reading/grid utilities) plus
story-only `mg-demo-article-*` sizing classes. "Editors' recommendations"
remains plain markup.
